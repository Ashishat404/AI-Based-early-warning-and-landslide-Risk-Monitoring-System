Community Field Reports

Residents, highway patrols and field volunteers can submit ground-level photos and short notes from a site — cracks, seepage, debris on the carriageway — to support the ML risk picture with on-the-ground observation.

Demo build: submissions and photos are stored only in this browser (localStorage), not on a server, so they are private to this device and are not shared with other users. A few placeholder "Sample" reports are pre-loaded to show the layout.
